# Nachfolgeradar (first wedge)

**Stage:** killed · **Platform:** web · **Score:** 5/5 · **Spend:** $0.65

Saved buyer searches and alerts, a guided anonymous listing with an AI-drafted exposé, NDA-gated contact, an arithmetic price range from published KfW multiples, and a directory of existing boards with deep links. Segment: Skilled-trade and small local service firms in Bavaria with revenue under about EUR 2M. This is the first wedge of a larger bet, "Nachfolgeradar": A self-serve marketplace where owners of very small German firms, starting with skilled trades, list a business anonymously and buyers save searches and get alerts. Build only the wedge, and keep the data model and the pages ready for the loop that makes it grow: Each listing gives saved-search buyers a reason to stay, each buyer makes listing more worthwhile for the next owner, and voluntary closing prices build a price benchmark nobody has.

**Why now:** standing need. Window: KfW reported in January 2026 that closure plans outnumber handover plans for the first time, and 57% of SME owners are now 55 or older. UniCredit's board member says the matching market is fragmented (March 2026). AI reduces the cost of preparing a listing.

## Alternatives found

- nexxt-change (partial): Free federal board with over 8,000 listings and 431 regional partners, but it reports 22,300 placements since 2006, roughly 1,100 a year against about 109,000 owners seeking a successor each year. How it counts placements is unknown.
- Deutsche Unternehmerbörse (DUB) (partial): Fewer than 1,000 listings and packages of several hundred euros per listing (search results; prices may be out of date).
- Dealsync (HVB) (partial): AI matching starts only after a client mandate and an M&A expert checks each match, which is costly for a very small deal.
